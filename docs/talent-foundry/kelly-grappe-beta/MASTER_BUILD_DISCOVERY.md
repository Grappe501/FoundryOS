# Talent Foundry — Kelly Grappe Campaign Beta

Status: Discovery / master build planning
Created: 2026-08-30
Target first hire: by 2026-09-01 campaign office opening

## Product thesis

Build the Kelly Grappe for Secretary of State internship/volunteer recruiting experience as Beta #1 of a reusable Foundry OS talent-discovery application. The eventual product should be configurable for Foundry/software-company recruiting and later for outside businesses through an employer onboarding system.

This is not intended to be a conventional application form. The candidate experience should reveal job-relevant evidence through choices, work samples, reflection, availability, follow-through, and other transparent interaction signals. Human reviewers make hiring decisions.

## Beta #1 immediate objective

Hire one paid intern at $20/hour as quickly as possible, ideally in place for the campaign office opening on Tuesday, September 1, 2026.

The campaign also wants to develop a volunteer talent bench. Applicants who are not selected for the initial paid role may opt into volunteering. Future paid openings may consider proven volunteers, but volunteering must never be represented as a guarantee of later paid employment.

## Discovery protocol

Steve is frequently dictating while driving. During discovery, ask one question at a time. Capture answers here so product decisions do not depend on conversation history. Do not start the final master build plan until the core discovery questions have been answered.

## Discovery Answer 01 — What the first intern actually needs to do

The first hire is effectively a lead intern / high-trust campaign operations assistant rather than a narrow task intern.

Operational responsibilities and desired capabilities described by Steve:

- Be highly dependable and immediately available.
- Be trusted to open and close the campaign office.
- Arrive on time and establish the office for the day, including basic hospitality such as having coffee ready.
- Configure and reset the office for different uses, from business meetings to social/campaign gatherings.
- Contact, coordinate, and mobilize other volunteers to needed locations and activities.
- Be technically proficient.
- Understand social media.
- Grow into the lead intern as additional interns and volunteers are added.
- Exercise strong practical judgment and make decisions within campaign doctrine and Kelly's established voice rather than substituting the intern's personal preferences for the campaign's.
- Maintain strong campaign focus.
- Function in practice as a high-trust operational extension of Steve, approaching a junior chief-of-staff role.

## Important assessment design decision from Answer 01

Do not announce that immediate availability is a preferred answer. Ask candidates neutrally, “When can you start?” and capture the factual response. Availability may be reviewed because this particular opening has an immediate operational need.

Do not create a hidden political-belief or ideological screening test. Assessment should focus on job-related capabilities, reliability, availability, judgment, communication, execution, and ability to work within an employer/campaign's established policies and messaging.

## Candidate ranking / decision guardrail

The software may organize applications and surface factual, job-related evidence for human review. Final political-campaign hiring decisions and any comparative candidate ranking should remain with authorized campaign humans rather than an AI-generated political preference score.

Beta #1: Kelly Grappe campaign paid-intern + volunteer pipeline.
Beta #2: Foundry/software-company talent pipeline.
Commercial product: configurable employer onboarding + reusable recruiting/assessment experience + candidate evidence workspace + talent bench/pipeline.
