# Company Foundry — Experience Blueprint

**Status:** Phase 0 + 0.5. The product is optimized to make the right person **fascinated with the workshop**, not to complete an application.

Campaign question: **Will you show up?**  
Company question: **What can you build, improve, lead, and multiply?**  
Thinking-trace question: **How did they work when the thing was still unfinished?**  
Internal product (0.5): a **talent-to-ownership academy and operating environment**. Never say that at the door.

The workshop records a flight recorder of moves (see `THINKING_TRACE_MODEL.md`). It does not type the person.

---

## Psychological arc

```txt
MYSTERY
  → CURIOSITY
  → AGENCY
  → COMPETENCE
  → INVESTMENT
  → RECOGNITION
  → BELONGING
  → CHALLENGE
  → CREATION
  → FEEDBACK
  → IMPROVEMENT
  → TRUST
  → RESPONSIBILITY
  → LEADERSHIP
  → GENERATIVITY
```

Generativity is the flywheel end-state: **I now help create the next person.**

Participant realizations (in this order, over time — not one sitting):

1. Wait. They're watching how I work.
2. Wait. I could actually build something here.
3. Wait. There is a method here — and they will show it to me.
4. Wait. There are other people doing this.
5. Wait. This could become paid work.
6. This isn't merely a hiring funnel. They're building the company out of the people who come through it. I might be one of them for a long time.

---

## Mystery doctrine

The company experience stays mysterious **much longer** than Campaign Foundry.

### Forbidden at the door (and for several meaningful interactions)

- Direct-to-camera founder video
- Founder introduction
- Company history dump
- Conventional careers page
- “We're hiring!”
- Résumé upload
- Job title menu
- Salary bait
- Giant process explanation
- Corporate values wall
- Enumerable `KEY 01` / `KEY 02`
- Intent picker that names intern / employee / partner
- Any sentence that says this is an application
- Academy / bootcamp / internship funnel language
- Course catalog, module list, Oscar, GitHub login wall

### Opening feel

**A strange digital workshop has noticed that I walked in.**  
Not: **I opened a job application.**

The product discloses itself through progress. Do not explain everything with paragraphs.

## Reveal map — when the participant learns what

| Revelation | Earliest stage | How they learn | Still hidden |
|---|---|---|---|
| Something noticed them | Stage 1 Discover | Opening copy + ENTER | Everything else |
| Their attention matters | Stage 2 Notice | “What do you notice?” acknowledged without judgment | Name of system |
| Their judgment has consequence | Stage 3 Solve | One decision → a result. Not told if “correct” | Company, jobs |
| **This is Talent Foundry** | After Stage 3, before or as Stage 4 begins | First revelation: “We don't start with résumés. We start with the work.” | Company identity, hiring, pay, ownership |
| They can **make** something | Stage 4 Make | Interest routing + first artifact | Who we are |
| **How we make things here** | After first artifact | “You made something. Now we're going to show you how we make things here.” | Company, jobs, Oscar |
| The work can be **remembered** | Stage 5 Remember | “YOU’VE LEFT SOMETHING BEHIND.” | Hiring pitch |
| The workshop **continues** | Stage 6 Return | A deeper challenge appears | Cohort, pay |
| **A company exists** and this is its workshop | Stage 6–7, only after return work has started | Indirect: tools, unfinished diagrams, internal names on artifacts — not an About page | Jobs, salary, equity |
| **Other people** are in here | When they meet a real participant (after training missions; fictional Rafi is not this) | Another bench. Shared work. | That walking in earned a cohort seat |
| **Hiring / placement opportunities exist** | Stage 9–11, after delivery + feedback | Human-authored invitation, never an auto banner | Compensation amounts as bait |
| **A named cohort exists** | When a human invites them | Explicit invite to FOUNDry Cohort 01 (3–5 seats, work starts **2026-11-15**). Not on the public door. | That walking in earned a seat |
| **Paid opportunities exist** | Stage 13 gate | Explicit human offer + agreement. Copy may say work can become paid **only** as a factual staff-initiated conversation | Equity |
| **Ownership / partner path exists** | Stage 14 | Separate founder conversation after sustained value. Foundry preserves evidence; it does not offer equity | Nothing is “earned” by finishing stages |

If a screen would make the method turn, GitHub, Oscar, or company identity appear before revelations 1–3, it is wrong.

## Stage map (14 stages)

The participant does **not** complete this in one sitting. The Foundry grows over time.

| Stage | Name | Public feel | What we observe | Identity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | DISCOVER | Curiosity. Dark workshop. ENTER | Did they enter? Dwell? Return? | Anonymous |
| 2 | NOTICE | What do you see? | What they notice; language; attention | Anonymous |
| 3 | SOLVE | One decision in a mess; a consequence | Judgment, tradeoff, what they ignore | Anonymous |
| 4 | MAKE | Small real artifact on a chosen track | Whether they finish; quality of making | Anonymous |
| 5 | REMEMBER | You’ve left something behind | Whether they want continuity | Optional identity |
| 6 | RETURN | Deeper challenge waiting | Follow-through after time passes | Remembered or still anonymous |
| 7 | COLLABORATE | Work involving other people | How they communicate and share load | Remembered (required for real people) |
| 8 | RESPOND | Feedback arrives | Whether they incorporate it — not whether they agree | Remembered |
| 9 | DELIVER | Definition of done | Completion vs abandon | Remembered |
| 10 | MULTIPLY | Help another participant succeed | Generativity | Remembered |
| 11 | LEAD | Own an outcome with people / time / resources | Responsibility under constraint | Remembered |
| 12 | BUILD | Supervised Foundry work | Trust with real (bounded) systems | Remembered + human authorize |
| 13 | EARN | Paid work | Only after human decision + agreement | Remembered + legal |
| 14 | OWN | Long-term ownership conversation | Separate legal/business decision | Remembered + legal |

Stages 12–14 are **never** auto-advanced by the software.

## Opening experience (Phase 1)

Black / dark / cinematic. Minimal language.

```txt
YOU FOUND THE WORKSHOP.

[pause]

Most people tell us what they can do.

[pause]

We'd rather see it.

[ENTER]
```

Do not explain the company. Do not show a logo lockup that says Talent Foundry or FoundryOS.

---

## First interaction — NOTICE (Phase 1)

Observational. Not political. Not employment-specific.

Show a flawed interface, process, message, product decision, or system (fictional or sanitized internal fragment — never production secrets).

Ask:

**What do you notice?**

Allow typing. Voice is desired later (campaign PL-5); text is sufficient for Phase 1.

Then:

**What would you change first?**

The system acknowledges without judging. We are creating curiosity → agency → investment.

---

## Second interaction — THE MESS (Phase 1)

An imperfect situation. Examples (pick one for Phase 1; do not build a library):

- confusing product screen
- messy customer feedback
- incomplete launch plan
- contradictory requirements
- small broken workflow
- unclear instructions
- operational bottleneck

Ask them to make **one** decision. Reveal a consequence. Do **not** tell them whether it was correct.

This is the company analogue of campaign Layer 1 judgment — without campaign content, keys, or volunteer framing.

---

## First revelation (end of Phase 1 / start of Phase 2)

Only after they have already interacted:

```txt
THIS IS TALENT FOUNDRY.

We don't start with résumés.

We start with the work.
```

Still do not fully explain the company.

## First artifact + interest routing (Phase 2)

Campaign Foundry primarily gathers **decisions**. Company Foundry must gather **creation**.

Small enough to finish. Real enough to reveal ability. Participant chooses what attracts them. This is **interest routing**, not personality typing. Labels are not assigned automatically.

| Track | Prompt |
|---|---|
| BUILD | Fix or improve a small interface or logic problem |
| EXPLAIN | Turn complexity into something another person can understand |
| INVESTIGATE | Research a small real-world problem and return evidence |
| DESIGN | Improve a workflow, visual hierarchy, or experience |
| OPERATE | Turn a messy objective into an executable plan |
| GROW | Find a credible way to get a product or message in front of people |
| ORGANIZE | Turn people, resources, and deadlines into coordinated action |

Tracks may be renamed in design. Do not show “you are a Designer” after they pick DESIGN.

---

## Discovery language (not keys)

Do not automatically call anything Key One.

Campaign lesson: `KEY 01` telegraphs `KEY 02`.

Company options to explore in design (Phase 1 may use none; first “opening” can wait until artifact or return):

- ACCESS GRANTED
- THE WORKSHOP OPENED SOMETHING
- A drawer unlatches
- A previously dark pane gets power

Progression should feel like the room changed — not like a collectible counter.

---

## Identity checkpoint (Phase 2)

Keep anonymous participation longer than Campaign Foundry.

After enough work that leaving would sting:

```txt
YOU'VE LEFT SOMETHING BEHIND.

Want us to remember it?

[ REMEMBER ME ]    [ KEEP EXPLORING ]
```

Remember Me fields:

- first name
- last name
- email
- phone only if genuinely useful (default: omit in Phase 2 unless a human follow-up path exists)
- location
- school / current work optional
- availability **later**, not at this checkpoint
- **no résumé**

Keep Exploring leaves the artifact on the local session. They may hit this checkpoint again.

Kernel: `remember/engine.ts` + `REMEMBER_COPY`. No public screen until Phase 1 UI exists.

---

## SAVE MY PLACE (Phase 2+)

For **remembered** Company Foundry participants only (same doctrine as V1.2: identity first).

- Cross-device opaque link
- No password
- No traditional account
- Company token space and table (see parallel architecture)
- Offer after Remember Me and at later natural rest points (return, after delivery)

## Visual evolution

The workshop should physically seem to open as the participant progresses.

| Moment | Visual |
|---|---|
| Early (1–3) | Dark, minimal, unknown |
| After first work (4–6) | Traces of machinery, grids, workbench, unfinished diagrams. Method turn. Room can grow a pane. |
| After setup / training repo | Repo / branch / log / schema as objects on the bench — not a course nav |
| After return / collaborate (7–11) | Workshop, projects, people, tools. Another person only after they exist. |
| After human gates (12–14) | Cohort, company, real work surfaces |

**THE PRODUCT DISCLOSES ITSELF THROUGH PROGRESS.**

## Share / virality

Highly shareable. Mystery drives it. Shared link **always** starts a fresh participant.

Campaign copy (do not reuse): *Know someone who should see this?*

Company variants to test (not rank, not score):

- WHO ELSE WOULD FIND THIS?
- SOMEONE YOU KNOW BELONGS IN HERE.
- DON’T EXPLAIN IT. SEND THEM THE DOOR.

Rules:

- No referral scoring
- No hiring advantage for referrals
- No spam prompts
- Company QR ≠ campaign QR

First share beat: after the first revelation or after the first artifact — **not** at the opening, and **not** mechanically after KEY 01.

## One-sitting vs over-time

| Visit | Stages | Target feel |
|---|---|---|
| First walk-in | 1–3, maybe glimpse of 4 | Fascination. They almost understand. |
| First making | 4–5 | I made something. They might remember me. Then: how we make things here. |
| Return + training missions | 6–9 | Clone, branch, broken build, inspect data. Still maybe alone. |
| Cohort | When they meet someone; then 10–11 | There are others. |
| Real work | 12–13 | This is the company. |
| Ownership talk | 14 | Rare, human, legal. |

Campaign V1.1 taught: finish a first visit cleanly. Company should also allow **leave fascinated** without forcing Remember Me.
