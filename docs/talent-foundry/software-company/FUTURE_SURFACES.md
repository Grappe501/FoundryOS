# Company Foundry — Future Surfaces

**Status:** Waiting-pass B. Design the strange product now so Phase 1–7 do not accidentally become a prettier ATS.

These surfaces are **not** Phase 1 scope. They are why the thinking trace and scenario lab exist. If a later slice cannot support these, the slice is too small or too conventional.

---

## 1. The transparent tape

The participant can eventually see the same flight recorder staff see.

Not a badge wall. Not “your thinking style is Architect.”

A timeline: *you sat with the door for twelve seconds; you marked Delete first; you never opened Import; you asked who the draft belongs to.*

This is how we stay futuristic **and** ethical. Secret psychometrics are yesterday’s product. A shared tape is tomorrow’s.

Phase 1 implication: do not store anything we would be ashamed to show them later.

---

## 2. The room is alive, not scored

The workshop changes because of **facts**, not points.

| Fact on the tape | Room may |
|---|---|
| They finished NOTICE | A second pane gets a faint grid |
| They chose `ask` | A drawer shows a blank question card |
| They returned after a day | The bench light is already on |
| They revised after feedback | The object shows both versions |
| A human invited them to a cohort | Other benches appear |
| They finished a training-repo mission | A repo / branch / log can sit on the bench |
| They opened a PR | The PR is an object, not a link dumped in a form |
| They met another participant | The cohort begins to exist in the room |

Never: XP, levels, KEY 02, “thinking score unlocked the east wing.”

The product discloses itself through progress. The room *is* the progress bar.

---

## 3. Voice as thought, not as application field

When voice lands (campaign PL-5 analogue): they are thinking out loud at the work.

Store the transcript as `notice` / `explain` words. Do not store “confident voice / filler words / accent” as signals. No emotion recognition.

---

## 4. Interview from the tape

Staff AI (Phase 6) may only propose questions that **cite events**:

> You asked who the draft belongs to. What did you need to know before you would have continued?

Forbidden: “Tell me about a time you demonstrated leadership” with no tape citation.

---

## 5. Return as a first-class signal

Campaign compressed Visit One so people finished. Company should also treat **coming back** as thinking: they left the work on the bench and re-entered.

Record `return` + gap. Do not treat a long gap as failure. Some of the best work arrives on the second walk-in. SAVE MY PLACE exists so the bench survives the gap.

---

## 6. Collaboration as a second tape

Stage 7 is not two people filling a form. It is two traces overlapping on one object.

Store: who handed what, who revised whom, who left a handoff. Humans judge whether that pair created value. Software does not pair “visionary + operator” types.

---

## 7. The command center is a workbench

Not an applicant inbox with stars.

Funnel + **open artifact** + **thinking strip** + **human decisions**.

If a reviewer can rank without opening the tape, the UI has failed.

---

## 8. Employer Foundry inherits the tape, not the campaign funnel

When Phase 8 productizes, the commercial object is:

- a mysterious door
- instrumented work
- a thinking trace
- human gates

It is not “Kelly config with your logo.” Designing the tape now is what makes that product real later.

---

## 9. The room becomes the development environment

Phase 0.5: `/workshop` is not a marketing wrapper around an LMS. Setup, clone, broken build, schema inspection, and GitHub facts happen **in the same room** that started as a puzzle. See `OPERATING_ENVIRONMENT.md`.

Phase 1 must not paint a finished ending that cannot grow those objects.

---

## What would make this *less* futuristic (reject)

- Adding a founder video so people “understand”
- Adding a job menu so we get cleaner funnels
- Adding a fit score so staff can sort
- Adding KEY 01 so progression is obvious
- Recording the face
- Typing people after the first artifact
- Optimizing time-on-site as success
- Teaching the method as a conventional course catalog
- Putting Oscar or “academy” on the first screen
- Requiring GitHub to ENTER

Fascination + a readable tape + human judgment is the future version of hiring. Speed-to-apply is the past version.
