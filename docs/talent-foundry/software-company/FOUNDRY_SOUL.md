# Foundry Soul Engine

**Status:** 1.0 — learning environment live under Company Foundry. Not a chatbot.  
**Code:** `apps/platform/lib/talent-foundry/implementations/company/soul/`  
**Held:** Remember Me, GitHub, Cursor, Oscar, cohort exposure, production, Phase 2B visual polish.

We are not designing an interface with personality. We are designing **a place capable of forming a history with someone.**

Phase 2B stays on visual HOLD. The room has architecture. This slice gives it **history, learning behavior, and relationship.**

---

## Doctrine

```txt
FoundryOS does not teach people answers. It teaches them how to go get better answers.
```

Never create the school instinct: “Am I allowed to use ChatGPT?”

```txt
Use everything you have. Then show us how you used it.
```

Culture:

```txt
Use every tool you have. Bring back what you learned. Show us what changed.
```

That is not a door poster. It is how the bench behaves.

---

## What we are measuring

Not whether they knew the answer.

**How did they find it? What did they trust? What did they reject? What did they test? What changed their mind? How did they use AI? Where was AI wrong? What did they bring back into the work?**

No scores. No badges. No token logging. Tool selection is itself a skill. We do not privilege ChatGPT, Claude, docs, or a person.

---

## Persistent intelligence environment

ChatGPT is an intelligence interface: **question → answer**.

FoundryOS is not trying to be a better chat box.

```txt
problem → environment → history → research → AI → human collaboration → artifact → consequence → reflection → increased capability
```

AI lives inside that loop. Chat is one possible tool.

---

## Research loop

Underneath nearly every mission:

```txt
Encounter → Wonder → Search → Compare → Test → Bring Back → Apply → Explain
```

The room may say:

- Find out what you need.
- You don't have enough information yet.
- Go look.

Then it gives them space to leave. The work stays.

When they return, it does not ask for a bibliography. It asks:

- What did you find?
- What changed because of it?
- What did you use?

---

## Four systems in 1.0

| System | Job |
|---|---|
| Research Return | Leave work, look, come back with what they found |
| Tool Receipts | Voluntary notes on how they used AI, docs, tests, people |
| Environmental Memory | Fragments accumulate beside the artifact |
| Soul Responses | Say nothing, reveal, ask one question, acknowledge, expose capability |

No chatbot. No Oscar. No fake personification. The Workshop itself is the first personality.

```txt

```

## Personality

Observant. Restrained. Curious. Consequential. Slightly mischievous. Demanding. Generous. Alive.

It notices meaningful work. It does not clap. Sometimes **silence is the response.**

## Loops

Soul:

```txt
Curiosity → Agency → Friction → Discovery → Attempt → Competence → Consequence → Recognition → Increased Capability → New Mystery
```

Capability Reveal still stands. See `CAPABILITY_REVEAL_ENGINE.md`.

The next emotions after the first ten:

Curiosity about capability. Productive uncertainty. Permission. Discovery. Competence. Recognition of the work (not the performance). Belonging. Trust. Identity through evidence.

The system never says “You're talented.” Their brain concludes it from accumulated evidence.

---

## Variable discovery, not variable rewards

The uncertainty is **what will I discover?**, not **will the machine give me my reward?**

Environmental rewards only: prediction error, competence, agency, curiosity gap, progressive mastery, real social recognition, meaning, memory.

No slot machine. No streak. No time-on-platform.

---

## Ethical rule

FoundryOS optimizes for **capability and voluntary return — not time-on-platform.**

Never punish leaving. Never manufacture urgency, streak loss, social rejection, or fake human messages. Never withhold a necessary fact to increase engagement. Never infer psychological vulnerability. Never score research. Never log tokens. Never privilege one tool.

They should want to return because they have unfinished work here, something they made matters here, people they respect are here, or they become better here.

## 1.0 on the linger bench

- During the first attempt, the room may say “Go look.”
- They can leave. The work stays. “The work is still on the bench.”
- On return: “What did you find?” Finding is enough. The rest is optional.
- Tools they name become receipts attached to the work, not profile badges.
- Fragments appear beside the artifact. A scratch. An older join. Time, not decoration.
- After a thin first artifact, the method turn stays. Then: “You fixed the symptom.”
- If they used AI and documentation, the room may later say that as a fact.
- Default response remains silence.
- Linger still says they can go.

Remember Me is still held. Identity is still not the relationship.
