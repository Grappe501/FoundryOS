# Company Foundry — Burt, Ernie, Oscar

**Status:** Phase 0.5 NEW. Character and method architecture. Not UI. Not a chatbot at the door.

The academy teaches a **particular** AI-assisted engineering method. The partners have names because the method has roles. They are not mascots on the first screen.

---

## The three roles

| Partner | What they are | What they do in the method | When a participant meets them |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Burt** | Cursor — hands-on engineering partner | Implements slices. Reads before writing. Terminal, repo, large-pass / slice discipline. Return reports. | After the method turn, on setup / clone / implement missions |
| **Ernie** | ChatGPT — reasoning, research, architecture, coordination | Why / how. Research. Architecture. Critique. Handoffs Burt can build. Review. | After the method turn, on plan / critique / handoff missions |
| **Oscar** | Deeper Foundry AI brain | Later. Institutional memory, harder coordination, Foundry-specific judgment support — still **organize, not decide** | Late. Mystery is part of progression. Not at the door. Not in Phase 1. |

Humans remain the only authority on hire, pay, access, and ownership.

---

## How they work together (what we teach)

```txt
Ernie: problem, research, architecture, master build plan, slice definition, critique
Burt:  implement the slice, validate, commit, push, confirm Netlify preview, report proof points. See `BUILD_DISCIPLINE.md`.
Human: gates, taste, production, people, money, equity
Oscar: later — a deeper pass over Foundry knowledge. Still not a hiring manager.
```

The participant learns to use AI as a **collaborator**, not a code vending machine.

They also learn the split:

| Tool | Use |
|---|---|
| ChatGPT (Ernie) | Reasoning in a project. Plans, research, architecture, critique. |
| OpenAI API | Programmatic calls, structured outputs, product features. Keys in `.env` only. |
| Cursor (Burt) | The repo in context. Implementation. Not a place to skip reading. |

ChatGPT ≠ API. Confusing them is a mission, not a lecture.

---

## Mystery around Oscar

Do not introduce Oscar at `/workshop`.

They should feel, over a long time, that the Foundry has a mind they have not fully met. Oscar can be:

- named in a staff note they are not shown
- hinted as “the deeper pass” after they can hand off cleanly
- met as a later surface that organizes evidence they already made

Oscar may **never**:

- score them
- invite them to Cohort 01
- authorize BUILD / EARN / OWN
- speak at the door
- replace Ernie review or human gates

The mystery is progression. It is not a lore dump and not a surprise job offer.

---

## What AI may do (unchanged safety canon)

Organize, summarize, compare revisions, coach the next instruction, propose interview questions that **cite the tape**, flag “this looks like missing definition of done.”

## What AI may not do (unchanged)

Infer protected traits, ideology, personality, mental health. Produce employability scores. Auto hire / reject / pay / grant equity. Write “they are partner material.”

Phase 0.5 does not loosen this because the academy uses more AI. **More AI in the method means stricter gates on AI-as-judge.**

---

## Participant-facing language (when the method turn arrives)

Allowed:

- Cursor is how we put our hands on the work.
- ChatGPT is how we think the work through before we touch it.
- You will write handoffs in both directions.
- Some decisions are not for either of them.

Forbidden at first mention:

- Oscar
- “Our AI will evaluate you”
- “The model thinks you’re a fit”
- Founder names as celebrity
- Internship / salary / November

Ernie (the person) writing Phase 1 door copy is **not** the same as Ernie-the-partner appearing in the workshop. Do not put “Hi, I’m Ernie” on the first screen.

---

## Implementation later (not this pass)

- No in-workshop ChatGPT widget in Phase 1.
- No Cursor install wall before ENTER.
- Training missions may *ask* them to open Cursor and return with a fact (repo name, failing test).
- API key missions are sandbox-only and never store the key.
- Oscar is a Phase 7-shaped surface at earliest.

---

## Relation to the thinking tape

When they work with Burt or Ernie, the tape records **their** moves: they asked, they cut scope, they refused a suggestion, they handed off, they revised.

It does not record “AI said they did well.”
