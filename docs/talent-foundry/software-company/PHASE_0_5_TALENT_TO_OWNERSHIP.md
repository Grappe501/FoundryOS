**Status:** NEW (Ernie review). Docs only. No `/workshop` UI. No production migrations.  
**Date:** 2026-09-02  
**Branch:** `feat/talent-foundry-company-phase-0`  
**Rule:** Do not start Phase 1 screens until this pass is reviewed. Do not lock the product into the current Phase 1 spec as if it were the whole thing.

This is **not** a rewrite of Phase 0. Phase 0 recovered the mission, the 14-stage spine, the cohort/evidence/gate models, and the `/workshop` door. Phase 0.5 **expands what the workshop becomes**.

---

## What changed

Phase 0 specified a talent flywheel: discover overlooked builders, give them unfinished work, keep a thinking tape, develop them in cohorts, feed the strongest people into the company.

That still stands.

What we must not do next is **build Phase 1 as a short mysterious puzzle that ends at linger**, then later bolt on “and also they learn to code.”

Company Foundry is a **premium talent-to-ownership academy and operating environment**.

People do not merely apply. They enter a mysterious experience, demonstrate how they think, **learn the Foundry method**, form cohorts, work together through GitHub on actual Foundry products, progressively earn trust and responsibility, potentially get paid, lead subsequent cohorts, build businesses — and remain **potential future partners throughout**.

Onboarding is part of evaluation **and** education.

The public door still must not say any of that.

## What is NEW (this pass)

1. **Academy + operating environment**, not a recruiting funnel with homework.
2. **The website progressively becomes their development environment.** `/workshop` starts as a puzzle and later *is* the bench, the repo, the review, the cohort.
3. **Curriculum is missions, not a course catalog.** They do not enroll in “GitHub 101.” A mission breaks, a repo waits, a database must be inspected before they touch UI.
4. **The Foundry method is taught after they have made something**, not at the door.
5. **Burt + Ernie + Oscar** are named partners in the method. Oscar stays mysterious until later.
6. **GitHub activity feeds the Foundry record.** Branch, PR, review, revision, contribution — a living body of demonstrated work. Not a résumé.
7. **They remain potential partners the entire time.** Staff posture, never a public promise, never an auto-banner.
8. **Do not start coding current Phase 1** until Ernie reviews this architecture. The door spec remains useful; it must not lock the product into a one-sitting puzzle.

## Partner throughout (posture, not a prize)

Phase 0 already placed ownership at Stage 14 as a separate legal conversation. That stays.

Phase 0.5 adds the **relationship rule**: from the first remembered person onward, staff treat them as someone who *might* one day be a partner — intern, builder, lead, or not. The software never says “you are on the partner track.” Completing missions never writes `partner_eligible`.

What the software may store: work, tape, GitHub facts, mentor notes, human gates.

What humans may later open: paid project, internship, employment, leadership, ownership conversation.

## How the 14 stages still hold

The spine does not get a 15th stage called “Academy.” The academy **fills** stages 4–12 as the room becomes real.

| Stages | Academy meaning |
|---|---|
| 1–3 Discover / Notice / Solve | Mystery door. Unfinished work. Thinking tape. Still anonymous. **No method lecture.** |
| 4 MAKE | They make something small. Then the room can say: now we show you how we make things here. |
| 5 REMEMBER | Continuity. The bench can survive. Still no résumé. |
| 6 RETURN | A harder object is waiting. Follow-through. |
| 7–9 Collaborate / Respond / Deliver | Fictional partner first (Rafi). Later: real people, real notes, real definition of done. |
| 10–11 Multiply / Lead | Generativity inside a living cohort — after they have met another participant. |
| 12 BUILD | Supervised work on **actual Foundry products**, human-gated, sandbox first. |
| 13–14 Earn / Own | Pay and ownership remain human + legal. |

Setup missions (tools, clone, branch, broken build, inspect the database) **attach to stages 4–7** as work objects. They are not a parallel curriculum app.

See `ACADEMY_CURRICULUM.md` and `OPERATING_ENVIRONMENT.md`.
