Do not scan localhost or a deploy preview for launch.

## Checklist

1. Scan the QR on a real phone. Confirm the address bar is `foundry-os.netlify.app/talent-foundry`.
2. First paint shows **Something is already in motion** and **Begin** — not a blank black frame.
3. Tap Begin → Continue through the opening, type a short change, choose a willingness, skip or watch the Kelly slot, commit to helping.
4. Complete the required volunteer scenario and continue past optional doors.
5. At **Who are you?** use a clearly fake test identity if writing to RedDirt. If this is a visual-only pass, stop before **Step in**.
6. If you do step in: you must see **[Name], you’re in** only after the write succeeds. A retry line is correct when the campaign connection is down.
7. Continue through paid interest, availability, one contribution area, first mission, handoff.
8. Refresh once mid-journey. You should resume, not lose the thread. **Start over** (top right) resets this phone only.
9. Confirm buttons are thumb-reachable and text is not cut off in portrait.

If any step fails, do not hand out more QR codes until it is fixed.
